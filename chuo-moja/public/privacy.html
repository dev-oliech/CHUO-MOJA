How Chuo Moja handles your data

Operator: replace this box before launch. This is a plain-language starting template, not legal advice. Fill in the organisation name and contact, have a lawyer review it against the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, and register the organisation with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner if required.

Who runs this

[Organisation name], [address], [contact email].

What we collect

  • Your university email address, used only to send you a sign-in code and to confirm you belong to a Kenyan university. Other users never see it.
  • Your name, as you typed it. It is shown beside what you post.
  • What you post or choose to share: posts, Open Lines, questions, project ideas, role claims and similar content, with the university your email belongs to.
  • Role claims (the text you write when asking a moderator to confirm your role). Only moderators and you can read it. Do not include ID numbers.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data or show you advertising.
  • Live room chat is not stored. It disappears when the room closes.

Who can see what

Posts and listings are visible to other signed-in users. Moderators can remove content and confirm roles. The people who run the server can technically access the database, so they should be limited to people you trust.

Your choices

In Me you can download everything linked to your account and delete your account. Deleting removes your account and the content you posted.

How long we keep data

Until you delete your account. Sign-in codes expire after 10 minutes and sessions after 30 days.

Questions or complaints

Write to [contact email].
